Tips for a good photo
  • Shoot in daylight with the lights on.
  • Hold the phone level and get the whole wall in the frame.
  • For textures, use Fix edges → Corners to line the dots up with the wall's corners.
  • Colours on screen are a preview. Check a real tester pot before you buy.
  • Scanned textures by Poly Haven (CC0). Wall AI: SegFormer (NVIDIA, non-commercial) and Depth Anything V2 (Apache-2.0).
